# Lucky Deal Games 🎴

Free indie card games made for players who enjoy classic cards, quick decisions and a little bit of luck.

## 🃏 Lucky Deal Poker — Android

A fast five-card draw poker game featuring a Joker, strategic holds, escalating bets and a High/Low gamble round... and high scores :) 

**[Download Lucky Deal Poker on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Davlgo.BrexPoker)**

## 7️⃣ Lucky Seven — iPhone

A fresh take on the traditional card game Sedmica. Play solo or in teams, climb the leaderboards and test yourself in the seven-stage Challenge Run.
